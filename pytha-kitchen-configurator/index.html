PYTHA kitchen configurator

Drop a PYTHA project file (.pyo) to get an interactive 3D model with an automatic bill of quantities, resizable cabinets and opening doors & drawers. The file is read in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere.

  • Parts, assemblies, materials and loose hardware are listed automatically.
  • Prices use editable default rates in INR (per cm², per running foot or per piece).
  • Room items (walls, windows, room doors) and appliances are shown but kept out of the furniture quote.
